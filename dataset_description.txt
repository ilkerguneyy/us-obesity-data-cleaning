
Obesity Levels by State Dataset
=================================

This dataset contains simulated data about obesity levels in the United States. It includes 700 observations with 14 variables, as described below:
1. Year: (Continuous) The year of observation, ranging from 2000 to 2023.
2. State: (Categorical) The U.S. state where the observation was recorded.
3. AgeGroup: (Categorical) The age group of the individual, one of ['Children', 'Adolescents', 'Adults', 'Seniors'].
4. IncomeLevel: (Categorical) The income level of the individual, one of ['Low', 'Middle', 'High'].
5. Gender: (Categorical) The gender of the individual, one of ['Male', 'Female'].
6. DietType: (Categorical) The predominant diet type, one of ['High Fat', 'Low Fat', 'Balanced'].
7. ActivityLevel: (Categorical) The physical activity level, one of ['Sedentary', 'Moderate', 'Active'].
8. ObesityLevel: (Categorical) The obesity classification, one of ['Underweight', 'Normal', 'Overweight', 'Obese'].
9. BMI: (Continuous) The Body Mass Index (BMI), normally distributed around a mean of 27 with a standard deviation of 5.
10. PhysicalActivityHours: (Continuous) Hours of physical activity per week, ranging from 0 to 20.
11. CaloricIntake: (Continuous) Daily caloric intake in kilocalories, ranging from 1500 to 3500.
12. MedicalVisits: (Continuous - Integer) The number of medical visits per year, following a Poisson distribution with a mean of 2.
13. ScreenTimeHours: (Continuous) Average daily screen time in hours, ranging from 1 to 10.
14. StressLevel: (Continuous) Perceived stress level on a scale of 1 to 10.


This dataset is ideal for exploring patterns and relationships related to obesity levels in the U.S. over time and across states.
